Maintenance & access

Temporarily take everything offline to the public, or fully shut the platform down.

Lockdown modeBlock the public from reaching any of your apps and pages. Only you — the admin — can still get to this dashboard. Use it during maintenance, or if something looks wrong. Flip it back off any time.
Shutting the whole platform down. Lockdown still keeps this dashboard reachable for you. To stop everything — the dashboard included — do it from the server where you installed Astrodock:
cd astrodock && docker compose down  ·  bring it back with docker compose up -d
There’s no “destroy everything” button in here on purpose — one click shouldn’t be able to lock you out of the very tool you’d use to recover.

Danger zone

These can’t be undone. The real app asks you to type to confirm each one.

Destructive actions
Delete all backupsRemove every saved database copy. You won’t be able to restore after this.
Clear activity & audit logsErase the whole history of sign-ins, deploys, and setting changes.
Rotate the secret keyReplace the key that encrypts your stored secrets. Existing secrets are re-encrypted; a few may need re-entering.
Remove all apps & dataDelete every app, page, user, and their data. The platform itself stays installed — this is the closest thing to starting fresh.
